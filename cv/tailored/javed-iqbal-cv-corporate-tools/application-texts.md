## Why do you want to work here?

Because the work itself is the kind I have spent my career doing, and the posting describes it honestly. I build custom WordPress themes from scratch in PHP, SCSS and JavaScript, and I have never liked handing a client a site-builder theme when the job calls for real craft. Corporate Tools runs a WordPress-heavy environment and says so plainly, which tells me the front-end work here is treated as real engineering: performance, accessibility and maintainability included, not decoration after the fact. ## Tell us about the 2 company principles that resonate with you most

Root Cause, "Fix The Actual Problem", is the first. Troubleshooting is a large part of my work, and my rule has always been to trace a fault in a theme, plugin or custom code to its actual cause instead of patching the symptom and moving on. A fix that only hides the problem costs the client twice, so I stay with an issue until I understand why it happened.

Craftsmanship, "Pursue Unreasonably High Standards", is the second. I build themes from scratch rather than reaching for a standard site-builder theme, and I check every site after updates and launches before calling it done, on mobile and desktop. The standard I work to is simple: if I would not sign my name to it, it is not finished. That is slower on the day and faster over the life of a site, which is the trade I believe in.
