## Tailored CVs — rules

1. One new tailored CV per job, made for that job only.
2. Check that job's requirements one by one. Tailor to those requirements for the highest match.
3. Skills on the CV are NOT final. Add skills per each job's requirements from Javed's full WordPress mastery — he knows the full WordPress work and has done it all. The CV skills list is never a closed list.
4. Only real facts. Never invent a skill, a year, a company, a date, a project or a reference.
5. If a job asks for a skill and it is not certain Javed has it, confirm with Javed first. Never add it without his confirmation.
6. For junior roles, do NOT over-mention seniority. No "Senior", no "Development Manager", no 11-year lead. Never invent a lower experience number or false dates — tailoring is emphasis and selection, not invented facts.
7. Every tailored CV is lengthier, about 2 pages: longer detailed summary, Core Competencies section, more bullets per role.
8. Every tailored CV carries the real contact details, the portfolio link, and the References section.
9. PDF is the applying format. The Word file stays as the editable copy.
10. Javed approves each CV first, then he applies himself.
11. No CV reaches Javed without QA sign-off. After a CV is built, the QA agent (`skills/qa-review.md`) checks it independently from every angle — facts, US English, writing, format, job match, hiring impact, consistency, links, completeness — and only a PASS goes to Javed.
12. All CVs are written in US English. American spelling only (specialize, optimize, customize, inquiry, center, program, organization). No British spellings anywhere.
