### About Wealth Enhancement

Wealth Enhancement is an independent wealth management firm with an endless passion for enriching the lives of our clients. We continually seek to perfect our craft of personalized financial planning with our team-based Roundtable and UniFi processes that go far beyond the standard approach. We proudly provide unique financial plans and investment management services to over 90,000 households from our over 175 offices — and growing — nationwide.

Since 1997, Wealth Enhancement has tirelessly raised the standard of wealth management with specialized knowledge and more attentive service that helps every client craft their future. For more information, please visit www.wealthenhancement.com.

We have an exciting opportunity for a **Senior Drupal Developer** (Full Stack)! As a Senior Drupal Developer (Sr Web Developer) at Wealth Enhancement, you will design, develop, and maintain digital experiences that support prospect engagement, lead conversion, and client relationships. Working with Product, Design, Marketing, and Technology, you will translate business needs into reliable, secure, sustainable and scalable solutions using Drupal and Acquia. You will own complex development work from technical design through release, including custom functionality, third-party integrations, performance improvements, and ongoing support. This is a remote position.

This is a senior individual contributor role with no direct reports or staff management responsibilities. You will report to and work alongside the Senior Drupal Web Development Manager. You will apply strong technical judgment, contribute to architecture decisions, and provide guidance through code reviews and mentoring while remaining focused on hands-on development in a fast-paced, iterative environment.

This is a **fully remote** position.

In the spirit of pay transparency, we are excited to share the base salary range for this position is **$132,000.00 to $140,000.00**, exclusive of bonuses and benefits. We encourage you to apply and provide us with your compensation expectations when you do. We're big on open conversations, so, let's have one.

### Primary Job Functions

### Design and deliver complex technical solutions

- Solve complex business problems with maintainable, scalable solutions that can evolve with our business
- Evaluate technical approaches and explain trade-offs in performance, security, complexity, and maintainability
- Develop and document solution designs using diagrams, prototypes, and proofs of concept as needed
- Build and maintain Drupal APIs, data feeds, and integrations, including Salesforce, in partnership with Technology teams

### Own hands-on, iterative development

- Develop Drupal custom modules, themes, and reusable components that deliver responsive, accessible web experience
- Create appropriate automated tests and support functional testing and release validation.
- Write maintainable code, review changes, and resolve application defects, performance issues, and security vulnerabilities
- Independently estimate and deliver assigned work; communicate dependencies, risks, and progress
- Demonstrate completed functionality and incorporate feedback into subsequent development

### Partner with Product and business stakeholders

- Provide technical input to roadmap planning, backlog refinement, and measurable success criteria
- Explain technical concepts and recommendations clearly to business and technical stakeholders
- Translate marketing and website needs into practical Drupal solutions that improve engagement and conversion

### Improve development and deployment practices

- Maintain clear documentation for code, configuration, integrations, and technical decisions
- Document deployment and rollback steps; support releases and post-release validation
- Use version control, code reviews, and automated checks to support reliable delivery
- Resolve code conflicts and improve development workflows, tooling, and release processes
- Maintain development environments and support platform updates, security fixes, and ongoing reliability

**Provide technical guidance and strengthen team capabilities**

- Mentor peers and share Drupal expertise through code reviews, pairing, and knowledge sharing
- Help resolve complex coding, integration, performance, and security challenges
- Partner with the Product Owner to clarify technical complexity and break initiatives into deliverable work
- Recommend improvements to development standards and address technical debt in line with business priorities

**Improve Service Quality by Using AI and Digital Tools**

- Use approved AI and digital tools to simplify work, improve service quality, and increase efficiency, while validating outputs for accuracy, privacy, and business alignment
- Identify and escalate inaccurate information and knowledge gaps, or issues related to digital tools

### Education/Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in software engineering or a related field preferred, demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, or education
- 10+ years of professional web development experience, including substantial hands-on Drupal development
- Current or recent experience working with modern versions of Drupal (10/11)
- Strong experience developing and supporting production solutions on Acquia
- Demonstrated ability to independently design and deliver complex full stack web solutions
- Experience working with Agile methodologies and managing individual delivery commitments
- Strong knowledge of Drupal architecture, configuration management, caching, security, and accessibility
- Proficiency in PHP, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Twig, and SQL. Experience building and consuming REST APIs
- Experience in Single Directory Components and Tailwind
- Experience with Git, Composer, automated testing, deployment pipelines, and tools such as Jira
- Experience with Salesforce integrations, AWS, or AI-assisted development tools preferred
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience reviewing code and mentoring peers
- Demonstrated involvement in the Drupal Community
- Include your Drupal.org username in your application
