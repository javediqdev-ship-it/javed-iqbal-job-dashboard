## Role summary (paraphrased from the employer page)

A Web Developer role on a fully remote team, working directly alongside the current Senior Technical Lead over a transition period. Through shadowing, pair programming and structured mentorship, the developer gradually learns the internal systems and takes over day-to-day technical operations as the Lead moves to a part-time supervisory role. The employer hires for personality, resourcefulness and communication as much as technical skill: a self-starter who manages his own time, knows when to ask for help (timebox a problem, document what was tried, reach out with a clear question), and wants to understand server architecture, DNS routing and clean code practices, not just click buttons.

## Requirements (paraphrased)

- Solid foundational knowledge of HTML, CSS, PHP and WordPress architecture
- Basic understanding of web hosting environments, FTP and database management (MySQL/MariaDB)
- Experience building or modifying WordPress themes; Astra and Elementor experience is a huge plus
- Exceptional written and verbal communication; comfortable documenting work and asking clear, context-rich questions by chat and email

## Stand-out extras (paraphrased)

- Custom web application development (PHP/JS) from scratch
- Familiarity with other CMS or e-commerce platforms (for example Shopify, MODX)
- Agency experience managing multiple client sites
- Familiarity with AI-based development tools

Benefits named on the page: a medical insurance program, a retirement savings contribution program, and a flexible, high-performance work culture.
