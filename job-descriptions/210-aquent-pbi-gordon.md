- Strong proficiency in PHP 8.x and core WordPress development (including hooks, filters, custom post types, and REST APIs).
- Solid experience customizing and developing within WooCommerce.
- Proficiency with front-end technologies including JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.
- Experience managing content, pages, forms, or templates in HubSpot.
