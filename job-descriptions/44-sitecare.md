We're looking for an experienced WordPress Developer to support ongoing development, optimization, and troubleshooting across a portfolio of complex WordPress websites.

- Build and extend custom themes, plugins, and blocks

- Translate Figma designs into production-ready WordPress implementations
- Collaborate closely with account managers and other developers

- Experience with WooCommerce and complex plugin ecosystems
- Strong debugging and problem-solving ability
- Experience working with Git-based workflows

Communication (Required)

- Excellent written and spoken English
- Ability to communicate clearly and professionally in a remote, async-first environment
- Comfortable participating in client-facing or internal discussions when needed

Technical Experience

- Performance optimization (Core Web Vitals)
- Familiarity with tools like Cloudflare, Nginx, MariaDB/MySQL, Redis
- Experience with build tools (NPM, Gulp, Webpack)
- Ability to work from Figma designs to production

Working Style

- Reliable, deadline-oriented execution
- Comfortable working across multiple tasks and priorities
- Able to collaborate as part of a distributed team

### TOOLS & TECHNOLOGY
