## Full description (Built In, fetched 27 Nov 2026)

The Senior Web Platform Engineer builds and scales WordPress websites, integrations, and marketing technology systems. Responsibilities include web development, platform security, accessibility, CI/CD, performance optimization, e-commerce enablement, analytics, attribution, Salesforce connectivity, consent management, and data quality. The role partners with Marketing, IT, Legal, and business teams, mentors developers, leads technical standards, and supports conversion optimization, SEO, and reliable lead lifecycle management.

ClearCaptions (founded 2011, HQ Antioch TN, 273 employees) — FCC-certified telephone captioning provider for people with hearing loss; mission to improve the lives of seniors and their caregivers.

What This Role Does:
- Designs, develops, maintains, and optimizes WordPress websites, applications, themes, plugins, and integrations.
- Builds secure, scalable, high-performing web solutions using modern engineering practices.
- Troubleshoots complex technical issues and ensures performance, accessibility, security, and maintainability.
- Supports WCAG-aligned website accessibility and compliance practices.
- Sets development standards, conducts code reviews, and promotes scalable architecture.
- Mentors and supports web developers through clear technical guidance, code quality improvements, collaboration, and timely issue resolution.
- Serves as the technical bridge between Marketing, IT, and business systems.
- Improves platform reliability through CI/CD, automated deployments, and release management.
- Supports lead generation, digital campaigns, conversion optimization, A/B testing, and landing page performance.
- Supports e-commerce strategy and execution (payment gateways, checkout optimization, e-commerce-enabled experiences).
- Implements and maintains marketing technology, analytics, GTM templates, and server-side tracking (GTM, GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, conversion APIs, Server-Side GTM, Meta CAPI, first-party cookies, ITP/iOS).
- Designs and maintains integrations between WordPress, Salesforce, marketing automation platforms, and enterprise systems.
- Supports data synchronization, lead capture, routing, attribution, lifecycle management, automation, and data quality across websites, marketing platforms, analytics, and Salesforce.
- Optimizes Core Web Vitals, scalability, technical SEO, user experience, and platform performance.
- Implements and maintains privacy and consent management (OneTrust, Google Consent Mode v2, GTM-based dynamic tag blocking) for GDPR, CCPA, CPRA compliance.
- Leverages AI-assisted development tools and automation platforms.

What You Will Bring:
- High school diploma or equivalent required; bachelor's in computer science, software engineering, IT, or related field preferred.
- Minimum 5 years of experience in web development, software engineering, or web platform engineering.
- Advanced WordPress development experience, including custom themes, plugins, APIs, and integrations.
- Strong proficiency in PHP, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, CSS/SCSS, REST APIs, and Git-based workflows.
- Experience with CI/CD, automated deployments, Salesforce integrations, analytics, attribution, and marketing technology platforms.
- Understanding of data governance, customer data management, consent management, and privacy compliance.
- Experience with e-commerce platforms such as WooCommerce, Shopify, BigCommerce, or headless commerce.
- Hands-on system integration experience (APIs, data synchronization, cross-platform connectivity).
- Demonstrated ability to lead through influence and collaboration, including mentoring.
- Excellent communication, presentation, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred: marketing operations, demand generation, integration architecture, SQL, reporting, managed hosting, headless CMS, modern front-end frameworks, API-first development.

Compensation: $120,000–$139,000 plus 5% bonus. ClearCaptions does not offer sponsorship for work authorization — candidates must be authorized to work for any employer in the US without a current or future need for Visa sponsorship.
