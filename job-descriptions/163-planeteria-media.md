## Role summary (paraphrased from the posting)

Design and develop fully custom WordPress themes (no pre-built themes), build sites from Figma mockups in HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript/PHP, deliver responsive, accessible, performant sites meeting WCAG standards, build custom features and integrations with ACF, custom post types and WordPress APIs, work with designers, strategists and account managers, troubleshoot and optimize for speed, functionality and security.
