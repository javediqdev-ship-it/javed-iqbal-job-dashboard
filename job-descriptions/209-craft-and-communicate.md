## Full description (RecruiterContacts, fetched 27 Nov 2026)

Senior Web Developer & Designer — Build Websites That Help Families Find Their Next Home.

Craft & Communicate is a marketing and public relations agency dedicated exclusively to senior living. We help communities connect with seniors and families searching for the right place to call home.

We're looking for an experienced Senior Web Developer & Designer who brings strong technical development skills together with an eye for exceptional design. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys both the creative and technical sides of building websites. You'll help bring digital experiences from concept to launch while ensuring the finished product is visually engaging and built to perform.

What You'll Own — Website Development:
- Build and maintain responsive WordPress websites using established development standards.
- Translate approved designs into functional website experiences.
- Develop reusable templates and components that support efficient production.
- Implement website functionality, forms, and third-party integrations.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and identify reliable solutions.
- Support website migrations and new site launches.
- Ensure websites are built with performance and long-term maintainability in mind.
- Assist with technical SEO implementation and structured data requirements.

Website Design & User Experience:
- Design modern websites that align with client branding and business objectives.
- Create responsive page layouts and reusable design components.
- Apply UX principles to improve navigation and conversion opportunities.
- Develop designs that balance visual quality with usability.
- Work with Content and SEO teams to ensure page designs support user intent.
- Refine existing website experiences based on performance insights and evolving client needs.
- Maintain organized design files and documentation.

Website Quality & Ongoing Improvement:
- Perform thorough quality checks before websites or updates are released.
- Identify and resolve issues affecting website functionality or appearance.
- Support site performance improvements and Core Web Vitals optimization.
- Follow accessibility best practices throughout development.
- Support website security through approved maintenance and development procedures.
- Implement approved cookie consent controls and tracking-related requirements.
- Identify opportunities to improve existing websites as technology and user expectations evolve.
- Communicate technical concerns and project dependencies before they affect delivery.

What We're Looking For: equally comfortable in website development and design; understands how design decisions affect technical implementation; can independently solve challenging website problems; takes pride in polished work with minimal revisions; thinks about website performance and user experience; understands secure websites; can balance multiple projects; enjoys collaborating; curious about emerging web technologies.

Preferred qualifications:
- 5+ years of professional website development and design experience.
- Strong hands-on experience with WordPress, including custom themes or advanced site customization.
- Proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Experience using Figma or comparable professional design tools.
- Strong understanding of responsive design and modern UI/UX principles.
- Experience creating website layouts and translating designs into working pages.
- Familiarity with WordPress multisite environments or multi-location website portfolios.
- Understanding of technical SEO and website performance optimization.
- Familiarity with accessibility standards and secure website development practices.
- Experience troubleshooting WordPress issues and third-party integrations.
- Ability to work independently within established technical and design standards.
- A portfolio demonstrating both design quality and technical development experience.
- Agency experience or experience with senior living and healthcare organizations is a plus.

This is a senior hands-on position rather than a department management role.

Compensation: $30.00–32.00 per hour, based on experience. Position: Full-Time, Remote.

Benefits: medical insurance, paid time off, paid holidays, 401(k) with company match, dental insurance, vision insurance.

How to Apply: apply through the Craft & Communicate careers page and include your resume, pay requirements, and a portfolio of websites you've designed and developed. They are especially interested in examples where you were personally responsible for both the design and development of a website.

## Why it fits
