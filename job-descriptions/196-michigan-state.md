### Position Summary (Basic Job Function)

This is a project pay position. Provides a wide variety of consulting services including conference, seminar or workshop design/presentation; operations review and analysis; project design, completion, coordination or management; or related consulting services.

### Unit Position Summary

Earlier this year, the A-CAPP website underwent a significant redesign and platform update. While the site's overall structure, branding, and design have already been established, a post-launch review identified opportunities to improve accessibility, usability, functionality, content presentation, and long-term sustainability. The project is focused on refining and enhancing the existing website rather than undertaking a new redesign.

To support this effort, we are seeking **two complementary professionals**: one role focused on the technical implementation and development needs of the website (this posting), and a second role focused on accessibility review, testing, and compliance (see Website Content Editor & SEO QA). Together, these positions will help ensure the A-CAPP website is both technically sound and fully accessible to all users while creating a stronger digital experience for our diverse audiences.

**OVERVIEW**

The A-CAPP Center at Michigan State University is seeking an experienced WordPress developer to improve and optimize our existing custom-themed website hosted on Flywheel. We are seeking a developer with strong expertise in WordPress, Advanced Custom Fields (ACF Pro), PHP, CSS, analytics, and accessibility standards who can evaluate the current implementation, recommend improvements, and execute targeted enhancements that make the site easier for non-technical staff to maintain.

The objective of this project is to improve functionality, accessibility, and content management while preserving the site's existing visual design, branding, and overall information architecture whenever practical.

Examples of current concerns include navigation elements that are not properly mapped to related content, accessibility compliance issues, limitations in staff content editing capabilities, and opportunities to improve the usability of existing ACF Flexible Content modules.

**SCOPE OF WORK**

**PHASE 1: WEBSITE AUDIT & REMEDIATION PLAN**

**Scope:**

- Audit the existing custom WordPress theme, PHP/CSS codebase, ACF templates, plugins, dependencies, and integrations.
- Assess website accessibility compliance and identify areas requiring remediation.
- Evaluate navigation structure, content relationships, post mappings, and information architecture to identify usability concerns.
- Review SEO and analytics configurations to ensure proper implementation and continuity.
- Assess the content editing experience for Center staff and identify opportunities to improve usability within existing ACF Flexible Content modules.

**Deliverables:**

- Findings report
- Prioritized fixes and enhancements
- Provide documentation and recommendations for ongoing website management.

**Timeline & Compensation:**

Deliverables are due within **one week of project start**.

**Compensation: $2,000**

**PHASE 2: IMPLEMENTATION, QA & TRAINING**

**Scope**

Based on the approved findings and recommendations from Phase 1, and while maintaining consistency with the site's established design system and brand standards, the selected contractor will:

- Implement approved enhancements to website functionality, structure, and user experience.
- Remediate identified accessibility issues.
- Correct navigation, content mapping, and content relationship issues.
- Improve the content editing experience within ACF by developing user-friendly controls for commonly used formatting options, including:
  - Typography and font sizing
  - Spacing and layout controls
  - Alignment settings
  - Approved color selections
  - Other staff-friendly formatting options that reduce reliance on custom CSS or developer intervention
- Test all implemented changes across applicable templates and page types.
- Provide brief training and/or documentation supporting ongoing website maintenance by Center staff.

**Deliverables**

- Completed implementation of approved enhancements.
- Quality assurance testing and validation.
- Staff documentation and training.

**Timeline & Compensation:**

Deliverables are due within **two weeks** of A-CAPP's approval of the Phase 1 audit and remediation plan.

**Compensation:** $4,000

### Desired Qualifications

- Demonstrated experience developing and maintaining custom WordPress themes.
- Advanced experience with Advanced Custom Fields (ACF Pro), including Flexible Content layouts.
- Proficiency in PHP, CSS, JavaScript, and WordPress template development.
- Experience improving content governance and editing workflows for non-technical users.
- Knowledge of WCAG accessibility standards and remediation best practices.
- Experience working with website hosting platforms such as Flywheel.
- Familiarity with WordPress SEO, analytics integrations, and performance optimization.
- Strong documentation, testing, and project planning skills.
