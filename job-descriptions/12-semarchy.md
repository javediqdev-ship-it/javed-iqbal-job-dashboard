## Role summary (paraphrased from the posting)

Semarchy (enterprise data platform) wants a sole web engineer to own its marketing site end to end: build it, run it, and evolve it. Today the site runs on WordPress, with PostHog analytics. The engineer builds the reusable components and templates marketers ship pages from, improves the main conversion journeys (homepage, platform pages, trial, demo and proof-of-value requests), makes sure every form or signup is captured and routed to the right account and owner, and owns hosting, security, releases, performance, consent and accessibility. Core requirements named: production web engineering on a marketing site; reusable page building from a short brief without a designer; CMS experience (WordPress or similar); forms and CRM integration with accurate routing. Nice to have (any two): experiment-driven page improvement, headless CMS with React/Next.js/Astro, a thin backend or custom capture layer, interactive experiences, structured data for search/AI answers, a design system in Figma or code, a multilingual site, safely changing a live codebase's architecture.
