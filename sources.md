- Individual job link is REQUIRED. A search page or company page is not a job.
- Score every job 1-10 against Javed's profile (11 years WordPress,
  100+ websites, custom development, dashboards, WooCommerce, Elementor).
- Every qualifying job goes to the dashboard New Jobs page with a full
  detail page. Every checked job goes in seen-jobs.md (added / held / dropped
  with the reason). After every round, run backup_round.py.
